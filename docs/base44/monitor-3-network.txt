3. TWO CARDS side by side (stacked on phones):
   a. "What hosts were paid in epoch {last.epoch}" (only when the network has "last"):
      - big: last.rewards as short GNK; beside it, grey: "≈ $33k · to the
        {last.hosts} hosts that took part";
      - when last.payout is there: "{pct} of the {minted} the network minted
        for that epoch" (pct = payout.paid ÷ payout.minted, no decimals;
        minted as short GNK) and a bar filled to that share;
      - when payout.notPaid > 0, a part titled "Why not all of it" with up to
        two rows, a bold short-GNK amount on the left and a sentence:
        • when payout.nothing > 0: the amount payout.nothing, and "{n} hosts
          were paid nothing: they missed too many of their requests, or were
          left out of the epoch." (n = payout.nothingHosts; with 1: "1 host
          was paid nothing: it missed too many of its requests, or was left
          out of the epoch.")
        • when payout.rest > 0: the amount payout.rest, and "{n} hosts were
          paid part of their share: some of their weight did not pass the
          network's checks during the epoch, or one host was over the limit
          of 30% of all the weight." (n = payout.partHosts)
        then, small: "What is not paid is not shared out among the other
        hosts. It goes to the network's governance account, which the hosts
        decide on by vote."
      - a fold-out "How are hosts paid?": "Each epoch the network mints new
        GNK and shares it among the hosts by the weight their GPUs proved. A
        host receives it a slice at a time over the following 180 epochs,
        about six months."
   b. "GNK: how much there is, and where" (only when the network has "supply"):
      - big: supply as short GNK; under it "exist now, of the 1 billion
        there will ever be ({pct})" (supply ÷ 1,000,000,000 GNK, no decimals);
      - one bar for the whole 1 billion GNK, with coloured parts for the
        parts below, each as wide as its share of 1 billion, and an axis
        under it: "0" on the left, "{n} still to be mined" in the middle
        (1 billion − supply, short GNK), "1B" on the right;
      - the parts, from the votes' "money", each a row with its colour
        square, the name in bold, a grey explanation and the short-GNK
        amount on the right (leave out any that is 0):
        • Community pool (money.pool.ngonka) — "spent only when the hosts vote for it"
        • Community sale (money.sale[0].ngonka) — "sold for USDT to pay grants and bounties"
        • Governance account (money.govAccount.ngonka) — "pays compensation, when the hosts vote for it"
        • Everyone else (supply minus the three above) — "hosts' rewards, the founders' share and every other wallet"
        Until the votes have been read, the bar is one part (the supply) and
        there are no rows.
      - a fold-out "Where does GNK come from?": "The total is fixed at 1
        billion GNK. 200 million went to Gonka's founders and 120 million to
        the Community Pool at the start. The other 680 million are mined:
        each epoch the network mints new GNK for the hosts, and that amount
        halves about every four years, so the last of it is mined decades
        from now." and a link "Gonka's tokenomics paper" to
        https://gonka.ai/tokenomics.pdf (new tab).

4. TWO CHARTS side by side (stacked on phones), each in a card, as line
   charts with a grey scale on the left and days along the bottom. Pointing

   a. "Hosts, epoch by epoch", from network.past (oldest first): the line
      is "hosts"; the days come from "at". The box: "{hosts} hosts" and
      "Epoch {epoch} · began {when}" — "began about {when}" when est is
      true; when is the full time. Under the chart, small: "{first} hosts
      then, {now} now." (first = past[0].hosts, now = the number of hosts).
   b. "GPUs, epoch by epoch", from network.capacity: the line is "gpus".
      The box: "{gpus} GPUs took part", then that epoch's kinds largest
      first ("H100 280 · H200 232 · …", from "kinds"), then the epoch line as
      in (a). Under it, small: "{first} GPUs then, {last} in epoch {e}.
      Point at an epoch to see which kinds." and a fold-out "What does this

      that epoch. The hosts report their own hardware; the network does not
      check it. GPUs differ a great deal, so the mix matters as much as the
      number." and "The network keeps only each host's latest hardware
      report, so the past comes from Gonkascan's record, which it has kept

      with a link gonkascan.com (https://gonkascan.com, new tab).
      When capacity is empty: "Not collected yet. It fills in over the next
