PROMPT FOR BASE44 — Mine or buy: hosts report their own hardware
(no pictures; paste everything below and publish)

On /mine-or-buy, in the table of what each rig earns (the one with "Rent
below" / "Pays for itself"), add two things the app now shows:

1. ABOVE THE TABLE, a note in a thin bordered box, in normal (not grey)
   small text, with a link at its end:
   "Measured on the machines running each setup now. Hosts report their own
   hardware and the network does not check it, and setups and tuning differ,
   so what your rig earns can be different. Before you rent, look at the
   hosts that run it."  See every host →
   The link "See every host →" goes to /monitor/network#hosts.

2. UNDER THE TABLE, with the other grey lines: when earnings.json has a
   non-empty "leftOut" list, one more grey line:
   "Left out of these figures: {list}. Their weight per GPU is more than
   {leftOutAbove} times that of a typical machine with the same GPUs, so they
   cannot be the hardware their hosts report."
   where {list} is each entry of leftOut written as
   "{gpuClass} — machines: {machines}, hosts: {hosts}", joined with "; "
   (for example "H100 — machines: 14, hosts: 3; H200 — machines: 11,
   hosts: 3"), and {leftOutAbove} is earnings.json's "leftOutAbove" (3).
   When leftOut is missing or empty, leave the line out.

Add the new words to every language the site offers. Change nothing else on
the site.
