Step 5 ("Health check"):
"GNOT checks the server against everything Gonka needs. "GNOT creates your wallet on your own computer. Its 24 recovery words are shown once: write them on paper and never share them. Then send it a little GNK: 0.01 GNK is plenty to set up, and the node's network fees come from it too, a little each epoch. GNOT shows what hosts with one server paid last epoch: about 0.02 GNK on November 12, 2026, so 1 GNK lasts about 45 epochs. Gonka's guide suggests 10 GNK as a reserve that lasts a long time. GNOT watches the wallet live and sees the GNK arrive by itself; "How do I get GNK?" shows the ways."

4. FAQ — THREE ANSWERS
Replace the answers to these three questions with exactly this. The questions themselves, their order, and the other 29 questions and answers stay as they are.

a. "What does it cost?":
"GNOT itself costs nothing to use. What you pay for is the GPU server you rent and the Gonka network's own fees. Setting up takes about 0.001 GNK. Since Gonka's upgrade of 8 October 2026 (v0.2.16) a node also pays a small network fee for every Proof of Compute it takes part in, out of its wallet: in Gonka's own example about 0.04 GNK an epoch, more for a bigger node. Measured on the network on November 12, 2026, hosts with one 8-GPU server or less paid about 0.02 GNK an epoch (the highest paid 0.06), so 1 GNK lasts about 45 epochs. Gonka's guide suggests 10 GNK as a reserve that lasts a long time, and GNOT's wallet and Verify steps show what a node like yours pays."

b. "How much does registering cost?":
"Registering your node and giving it its permissions cost about 0.001 GNK in network fees together; send 0.01 GNK to be safe. Taking part is what costs: since v0.2.16 a node pays a fee for every Proof of Compute, out of your wallet. A node that cannot pay stays registered but is left out of the epoch and earns nothing. For one server that was about 0.02 GNK an epoch on November 12, 2026, so 1 GNK lasts about 45 epochs. Gonka's guide suggests about 10 GNK as a reserve, and the app shows whether your node has enough. "How do I get GNK?" shows where to get it."

c. "How do I vote on a proposal?":
"A host votes with its node, and the vote counts by the node's weight. Open Monitor/Vote in GNOT, link your node's wallet once, and vote on the Votes page. A node that is not in the current epoch can still vote, but its vote only counts if the node is in the epoch when voting ends. The app keeps a record of how you voted. The website shows every proposal and how the vote stands, but it never asks for your wallet, so voting happens in the app."
