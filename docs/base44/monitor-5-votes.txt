First, two small fixes to how Monitor/Vote writes amounts, everywhere in it:
   a. Short amounts below 10,000 are written in full, with separators, not
      shortened: "≈ $3,956", not "≈ $3.9k"; "≈ $8,895", not "≈ $8.9k". From
      10,000 up they are shortened, ROUNDED to the nearest, not cut off:
      102,654 is "102.7k", not "102.3k". This is the same rule for GNK and
      for dollars.
   b. Full GNK amounts drop trailing zeros: "1 GNK", not "1.000 GNK";
      "5.017 GNK" and "0.05 GNK" stay as they are.
