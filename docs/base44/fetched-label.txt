PROMPT FOR BASE44 — one clear "Fetched" label wherever figures come from the network
(no pictures; paste everything below and publish)

Visitors should see at a glance how fresh the live figures are. Add one small grey label, the same everywhere:

  "Fetched 5:24 PM · just now"
  "Fetched 5:24 PM · 3 min ago"

The time is in the visitor's own clock. Under a minute it says "just now"; after that the minutes or hours, and it keeps counting while the page stays open. If the reading is from another day, add the day ("Fetched Mon, 5:24 PM · 1 day ago"). Show nothing until the figures have loaded.

Where it goes:

1. MINE OR BUY (/mine-or-buy), near the top, right under "What each rig mined over the last epoch…". The time comes from the gnot-rentals answer: earningsAsOf. Use earningsAsOf, not the moment the page loaded, so a reading that is older says so.

2. MINE OR BUY, "Compare with providers", on the Spheron card: replace "Prices as of …" with the label, using that provider's "at" from the same answer.

3. GET A NODE (/get-a-node), "Right now at Spheron": replace "Read from Spheron's public price list today, 5:25 PM." with the label, followed by "Read from Spheron's public price list. Before you rent, check on Spheron's own site."

4. MONITOR/VOTE (/monitor and every page under it), in the top bar, just left of the Refresh button, one label for what the page shows: the time that page's data arrived from gnot-monitor (the node's answer on a node's page, the votes answer on Votes, the network answer elsewhere). On a node's page, remove "read just now" so there is only one.

Do not add the label to anything else: leave "Checked against the live Gonka network …" and "GPUs counted machine by machine at …" as they are.

Add the new words to every language the site offers. Change nothing else on the site.
