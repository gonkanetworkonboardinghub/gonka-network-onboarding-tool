3. FAQ (/faq)
   a. "What does it cost?" — new answer:
      "GNOT itself costs nothing to use. What you pay for is the GPU server
      you rent and the Gonka network's own fees. Setting up takes about 0.001
      GNK. Since Gonka's upgrade of 8 October 2026 (v0.2.16) a node also pays
      a small network fee for every Proof of Compute it takes part in, out of
      its wallet: in Gonka's own example about 0.04 GNK an epoch, more for a
      bigger node. Gonka's guide suggests starting with 10 GNK, which lasts a
      long time, and GNOT's Verify step reads from your server what your node
      will need."
   b. In WHAT YOU NEED, after "Can I rent a spot or interruptible server to
      save money?", a new question:
      Q: "How do I get GNK?"
      A: "Your node's wallet holds native GNK, on the Gonka network; GNOT shows
      its address. If you already hold GNK in a Gonka wallet such as Keplr or
      Tangem, send it there. If you hold WGNK on Ethereum (the traded form, on
      Uniswap), Gonka's bridge on its own dashboard turns it into GNK in about
      15–20 minutes, paid out to the gonka1 address of your Ethereum wallet;
      from there, send it to your node's wallet. The only official WGNK is
      contract 0x972a7a92d92796a98801a8818bcf91f1648f2f68. Start with a small
      amount: transfers cannot be undone."
      Under it, two links: "Gonka's wallet guide"
      (https://gonka.ai/docs/wallet/wallet-and-transfer-guide/) and "Gonka's
      bridge guide" (https://gonka.ai/docs/cross-chain-transfers/ethereum-bridge/dashboard/),
      both opening in a new tab.
   c. In WHAT YOU NEED, after the new one:
      Q: "What is an SSH key, and do I need one?"
      A: "It is how GNOT logs in to your rented server from your computer.
      Most providers ask for one while you rent. If you don't have one, GNOT
      makes it for you before you rent: open Gonka Host Setup, press "Create
      an SSH key" on the first step, and paste it into your provider's SSH key
      box. The private half never leaves your computer."
