1. DOWNLOAD PAGE ON PHONES — A FAULT, FIX FIRST
At phone width (360px, 375px) the Download page is about 1,090px wide instead of the width of the screen. The two cards (Windows, Mac) stretch to the full length of the command line, their text is cut off on the right, and the menu button in the top bar is pushed out of view.
Cause: the command sits in <code class="... whitespace-nowrap"> inside <div class="overflow-x-auto max-w-full">, but the boxes around it have no minimum width of zero, so the long line pushes them wide.
Fix:
- The grid "grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6": use columns of minmax(0, 1fr) (Tailwind: grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2, with min-w-0 on both cards).
- Add min-w-0 to each card ("border rounded bg-card/50 p-6 md:p-8 flex flex-col"), to the wrapper "relative space-y-8 flex-1" inside it, and to each step's row "flex gap-4".
- The command box keeps overflow-x: auto, so the long command scrolls sideways inside its own box and nothing else moves.
- After the fix, at 360px and 375px wide, document.documentElement.scrollWidth must equal the window's width on Download and on every other page.
Also on Download, on desktop: the small label "This computer" pushes the title "Windows" lower than the title "Mac". Keep both titles on the same line: reserve the label's height in both cards.
