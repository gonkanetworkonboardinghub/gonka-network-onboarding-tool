On /faq, keep the page's look and its sections. Change these answers and add
these questions (each new question goes in the section named, at the end of
it unless said otherwise). Keep everything else as it is.

A. CHANGE

1. "What is The Gonka Network Onboarding Tool (GNOT)?" — new answer:
   "An app for Windows and Mac for running a node on the Gonka network. It
   has two parts. Gonka Host Setup takes a rented GPU server to a registered,
   earning node without you typing command lines. Gonka Host Monitor/Vote
   follows any node — yours or anyone else's — next to every other host,
   shows the network as a whole and the proposals being voted on, and lets
   you vote with your own node. Monitor/Vote is also on this website, without
   the voting."

2. "Is it free?" — rename the question to "What does it cost?" and start the
   answer with "GNOT itself costs nothing to use." instead of "The app itself
   costs nothing." Keep the rest of that answer as it is.

3. "What hardware does a node need?" — new answer:
   "Right now: the GPUs of a setup Gonka supports (Mine or buy lists every
   setup and what it earns), a dedicated server — never spot or
   interruptible — memory of at least 1.5 × the GPUs' memory, 1,000 GB of
   disk, 16 or more CPU cores, Linux with an NVIDIA driver for CUDA 13 (or
   12.9), root login over SSH, and ports 8000 and 5000 open. The
   requirements change as Gonka adds models and GPUs, and GNOT checks your
   server against the current ones before it sets anything up. The Get a
   Node page has the full list." ("Mine or buy" links to /mine-or-buy, "Get
   a Node" to /get-a-node#needs.)

4. "How does a node earn?" — add at the end: "Each epoch's reward is paid a
   slice at a time over the next 180 epochs, about six months. Mine or buy
   shows what each setup earns now, measured on the network." ("Mine or buy"
   links to /mine-or-buy.)

5. "Does the app collect anything about me?" — the sentence "That is how the
   Impact page counts the people who have the app." becomes "That is how this
   website counts the computers that have the app."

B. ADD

6. In WHAT YOU NEED, after "Do I need to own a GPU?":
   Q: "Can I rent a spot or interruptible server to save money?"
   A: "No. A spot, interruptible or preemptible server can be taken back by
   the provider at any moment, and a node stopped partway through an epoch is
   paid nothing for that epoch — you would pay for the server and earn
   nothing. Rent a dedicated (on-demand) server. Mine or buy compares
   dedicated servers only."

7. In EARNING, COLLATERAL AND FEES, first in the section:
   Q: "How much will my node earn?"
   A: "Mine or buy shows what each setup earned over the last epoch, measured
   on the network, and its calculator tells you whether a server at your
   price pays for itself. It is a rough guide, not a promise: rewards fall
   as more GPUs join, a new node earns less at first, the GNK price moves,
   and what you mine arrives over about six months."

8. In EARNING, COLLATERAL AND FEES, at the end:
   Q: "Where do this website's numbers come from?"
   A: "From the Gonka network itself, read live with the same code the app
   runs, so the website and the app show the same figures. The GNK price is
   the middle of four markets: Uniswap (through GeckoTerminal), DEX
   Screener, CoinGecko and CoinPaprika, which your own browser reads when the
   page opens, as the app reads them from your computer. When the network or
   a market does not answer, the last reading GNOT published on GitHub, a few
   hours old at most, stands in."

9. In EARNING, COLLATERAL AND FEES, at the end:
   Q: "Are the network's GPU numbers real?"
   A: "The GPU count is what the hosts report, and the network does not check
   it. Some large hosts report far fewer GPUs than their weight takes, so the
   real number is higher. Monitor/Vote also shows the network's weight in
   H100s, which does not depend on what hosts report, flags the hosts that
   report too few, and Mine or buy leaves their machines out of its figures."
   ("Monitor/Vote" links to /monitor/network, "Mine or buy" to /mine-or-buy.)

10. A new section, MONITOR AND VOTE, after EARNING, COLLATERAL AND FEES, with:
   Q: "Can I follow someone else's node?"
   A: "Yes. In Monitor/Vote, My nodes holds any node you follow — your own or
   any other host's — by its address. On this website the list is kept in
   your browser only; in the app it is kept on your computer."
   Q: "How do I vote on a proposal?"
   A: "A host votes with its node, and the vote counts by the node's weight.
   Open Monitor/Vote in GNOT, link your node's wallet once, and vote on the
   Votes page. The website shows every proposal and how the vote stands, but
   it never asks for your wallet, so voting happens in the app."
   Q: "How do I stop my node safely?"
   A: "In GNOT, open your node in My nodes and choose Shut down safely: it
   takes the node off the network the way Gonka's guide says to, step by step
   on your server. Never delete the .tmkms folder on your server."
