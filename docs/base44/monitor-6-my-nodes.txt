Then build /monitor/my-nodes and /monitor/add inside the Monitor/Vote frame, as
the app shows them in the attached pictures (references for the layout only;
do not put them on the site). On the website, "my nodes" are the nodes a
visitor chooses to follow, kept in their own browser only: localStorage key
"gnot-monitor-nodes", a JSON list of { "address", "name" }, at most 50, no
address twice. No account, nothing sent anywhere. The figures on the cards
come from the network answer ({ "view": "network" }), so one reading covers
every card. Use the number rules from the earlier Monitor/Vote prompt.

1. MY NODES, WHEN THE LIST IS EMPTY (as in m06):
   - the My nodes icon, big, in its rounded square; the title "My nodes";
     the line "Add your node to follow it here.";
   - two buttons: a filled "Add your node" (→ /monitor/add) and "Find it
     among every host" (→ /monitor/network, scrolled to the "Every host"
     card, anchor id "hosts");
   - six small tiles in a grid (three across, two on phones), each with a
     padlock, a bold name and a grey line:
     • Status — whether it is in the epoch, and why not when it is not
     • Earnings — what it earned, next to a typical host
     • Fees and collateral — what is left for fees, and what is staked
     • Vote — on the network's proposals, with your node
     • Your server — a terminal to your server
     • Shut down safely — Gonka's steps for leaving, done for you
     The last three also carry a small tag "GNOT app", and clicking one of
     them opens the "Needs GNOT" window with its words (voting, the command
     line, shutting down).
   - under them, small and grey: "Nodes you add are kept in this browser
     only. Voting, the command line and shutting down are in the GNOT app,
     where your node's wallet and your server's login stay on your own
     computer.", with "the GNOT app" linking to /download.

   - the title "My nodes" and beside it, grey, "1 node" or "{n} nodes";
   - after a node was removed, a bar for 10 seconds: "Removed {name}." with
     an "Undo" button that puts it back where it was;
   - a grid of cards (three across on a computer, one on phones), one per
     node, each a link to /monitor/node/<address> (opened "from My nodes"):

       loading animation while the network is read; "In epoch {n}" (green)
       when its address is among the network's hosts; "Left out of epoch
       {n}" (red) when it is there with excluded true; "Not in epoch {n}"
       (red) when it is not there;
     • its name in bold (the short address when it has no name), and the
       short address under it in monospace;
     • three small figures: "Share" (its share as a percentage with 2

   - under the grid, small and grey: "Kept in this browser only. To vote
     with a node, use the GNOT app." with "the GNOT app" linking to
     /download.
