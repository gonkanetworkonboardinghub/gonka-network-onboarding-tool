2. FAQ (/faq), "How much does registering cost?" — new answer:
   "Registering your node and giving it its permissions cost about 0.001 GNK
   in network fees together; send 0.01 GNK to be safe. Taking part is what
   costs: since v0.2.16 a node pays a fee for every Proof of Compute, out of
   your wallet. A node that cannot pay stays registered but is left out of the
   epoch and earns nothing. Gonka's guide suggests starting with about 10 GNK,
   and the app shows whether your node has enough. "How do I get GNK?" shows
   where to get it."
