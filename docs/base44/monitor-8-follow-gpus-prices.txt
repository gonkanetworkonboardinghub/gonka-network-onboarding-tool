PROMPT FOR BASE44 — Monitor/Vote: follow any node, a clear Back button, what hosts report, and where the price comes from
(attach network-top-new.png and my-nodes-new.png from the folder
"monitor-update-pictures" — references from the app for the layout only, do
not put them on the site — then paste everything below and publish)

The app's Monitor/Vote changed in six ways; make the website's the same.
Everything reads the gnot-monitor function as now (its answers now carry the
new fields named below).

1. "MY NODES" IS THE NODES YOU FOLLOW — your own, or anyone else's. Reword:
   - Start page, My nodes card: the sentence becomes "The nodes you follow,
     yours and anyone else's: how they are doing and what they earn." When
     this browser follows no node, the big figure is "Follow a node" and the
     grey line under it "Yours or any other host's, by its address".
   - /monitor/my-nodes with no node (as in my-nodes-new.png): the line under
     the title is "Follow your own node, or any other host you want to keep
     an eye on."; the buttons are "Follow a node" (→ /monitor/add) and "Find
     one among every host"; the grey line at the bottom begins "Nodes you
     follow are kept in this browser only." (instead of "Nodes you add …").
   - /monitor/my-nodes with nodes: the last card is "Follow a node" with
     "Yours or any other host's, by its address". After a node is taken off,
     the bar says "Stopped following {name}." with "Undo" — and it shows on
     the empty page too, when the last node was taken off.
   - /monitor/add: the title is "Follow a node", the button "Follow", and
     the line under the form "Your own node or any other host's: any node
     works, however it was set up. The address starts with gonka1." (then
     the link to every host, as now). The browser tab: "Follow a node ·
     Monitor/Vote | The Gonka Network Onboarding Hub".
   - A node's page: "Add to your nodes" becomes "Follow this node", and
     "Remove" becomes "Unfollow". When "Rename" opens for a node without a
     name, its box starts empty (not with the short address).

2. THE BACK BUTTON in the Monitor/Vote bar ("← Monitor/Vote", "← The
   network", "← My nodes") is small and easy to miss. Make it the most
   visible thing on the bar, as in the pictures: a rounded pill with a
   1.5 px border in the text colour, a light background, the arrow at about
   18 px and the label at 15 px bold, padding about 10 px 20 px; on hover the
   background brightens and the arrow moves left a little. On phones it stays
   a full pill (it may drop the label to "← Back" if space is short).

3. VOTES: remove the "Ask for money" chip. The chips are "All", "Passed" and
   "Rejected", with their counts, as now.

4. WHERE THE GNK PRICE COMES FROM, on The network page's GNK PRICE tile (as
   in network-top-new.png):
   - the grey line under the price says "the middle of {n} markets" (n = the
     number of price.sources), instead of "from several markets";
   - under it, small, one line per market from price.sources: its name and
     its price with 3 decimals, e.g. "Uniswap (GeckoTerminal) $0.162",
     "DEX Screener $0.163", "CoinGecko $0.170", "CoinPaprika $0.170". A
     market that has "readAt" did not answer the website just now: add
     " · read {time}" after it (readAt as a time, e.g. "read 9:06 PM").

5. WHAT HOSTS REPORT ABOUT THEIR GPUs. Hosts report their own hardware and
   the network does not check it; the three largest hosts, with over half of
   the weight, report about 20 GPUs each. So:
   - The network page's GPU tile: the label becomes "GPUs hosts report, epoch
     {n}" (instead of "GPUs in epoch {n}"). Under "{working} working", when
     gpus.h100 is there, one line in normal text: "All the weight equals
     about {gpus.h100.equivalent} H100s". The kinds stay as they are.
   - Under the four tiles, when gpus.underReported.hosts is above 0, a
     warning box (the site's warning style): "Hosts that report far fewer
     GPUs than their weight takes: {hosts}, with {weightShare as a percentage,
     no decimals} of the network's weight. So the network has many more GPUs
     than are reported. Hosts report their own hardware, and the network does
     not check it."
   - The GPU chart: its title becomes "GPUs hosts report, epoch by epoch"; the
     pointing box says "{n} GPUs reported" (instead of "{n} GPUs took part");
     and its fold-out "What does this count?" gets one more paragraph: "Some
     hosts report far fewer GPUs than they run, so this line is lower than the
     real number, and it jumps when a host changes what it reports. The
     network's weight in H100s, on the GPU tile above, does not depend on
     what hosts report."
   - Every host: a host with underReported true gets a small outlined flag
     after its address, "reports too few GPUs", in the normal text colour
     (not red), with the tooltip "Its weight equals about {h100} H100s, far
     more than the GPUs it reports." On phones the flag sits under the
     address.
   - The start page's "The network" card: the grey line says "{n} GPUs
     reported · epoch {e}" (instead of "{n} GPUs · epoch {e}").
   - A node's page, "Hardware the network sees": right under the line "{working}
     of {n} machines working · …", when hardware.underReported is true, a
     warning box: "Its weight equals about {hardware.h100} H100s, but it
     reports {hardware.gpuCount} GPUs: it runs far more than it reports. Hosts
     report their own hardware, and the network does not check it."
     Otherwise, when hardware.h100 is there, a small grey line: "Its weight
     equals about {hardware.h100} H100s."

   on the site.
