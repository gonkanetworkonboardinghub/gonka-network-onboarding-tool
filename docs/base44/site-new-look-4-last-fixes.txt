1. REGISTER A NODE — THE ROW OF STEP NUMBERS IS HIDDEN BEHIND THE TOP BAR (a fault, fix first)
The row of 14 step numbers under "Step by step with GNOT" (<div class="sticky top-0 z-30 -mx-6 px-6">) sticks at top: 0. But the top bar (<div class="fixed top-0 left-0 right-0 z-50">) covers the first 86px of the window on a computer and 98px on a phone, and it sits above the row. So the moment the row sticks, it slides under the top bar and nobody sees it.
Fix:
- Stick the row directly under the top bar: its "top" must be the top bar's real height. Measure the top bar element, and measure again when the window is resized; do not hard-code it (today it is 86px on a computer and 98px on a phone).
- After a click on a number, the step's card must not end up under the row: the cards' scroll margin (now scroll-mt-24) must be the top bar's height + the row's height (58px) + 12px. Use the same offset in the test that decides which step is in view (today: getBoundingClientRect().top - 120 <= 0).
- After the fix, scroll through the 14 steps on a computer and on a phone: the row of numbers with its thin progress line stays visible under the top bar the whole time, and the number of the step in view is highlighted.

2. PAGES RISE IN WHEN THEY OPEN — MISSING ON MOST PAGES
Get a node does it right: the heading block and then each section rise in (the "reveal" wrapper, which gets the class "in"). The other pages do not match it:
- Nothing rises in at all on: Register a node, Download, Monitor/Vote → My nodes, The network, Votes, a host's page.
- Only the heading block rises in, not the sections, on: Mine or buy, FAQ, the Monitor/Vote front page.
Make every page behave exactly like Get a node: the heading block first, then each section, 60ms apart (no more than 8 steps of delay); sections further down the page rise in when they first come into view. Once, not on every scroll.
Two safety points:
- Wrap each section or card on its own, never a whole long list in one wrapper. The list of proposals on Votes is about 18,000px tall; the hosts table and the 14 steps are long too. The wrapper's IntersectionObserver uses threshold 0.05 today: a block more than about 18 times taller than the window can never reach that and would stay invisible for ever. Set the threshold to 0.
- Nothing may ever stay invisible. Under prefers-reduced-motion everything is simply there (the CSS already does this; keep it).
