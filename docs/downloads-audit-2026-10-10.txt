GNOT — WHO MADE EVERY DOWNLOAD
Audit of 10 October 2026. All 58 downloads GitHub has ever counted, matched one by one.

WHAT IT WAS CHECKED AGAINST
  1. GitHub's own logs of every automated run in the project (440 runs), step by step, with times.
  2. The record of all 12 work sessions, 6 July to 10 October: every command that was run.
  3. The publisher's computer: every update file its copy of the app downloaded is still in the
     temp folder with its time, plus the PowerShell history.
  4. Old readings of the counters: an exact one from 20 Sept 17:51 UTC, and about one an hour
     from 25 Sept 16:31 UTC onward (274 snapshots on the data branch).
  5. A test on 10 Oct: one "ping" (HEAD request, redirect not followed) at a private file's
     download link raised its counter from 1 to 2. So a ping counts as a download.
  The publisher told people about GNOT on 16 Sept at 11:00 AM Eastern (15:00 UTC).

WHAT THIS MEANS
  - Since 20 Sept 17:51 UTC, every single download is the publisher's own computer, a robot, or a
    test. No other computer has installed or updated the app in that time.
  - In the whole history there are at most 2 downloads by anybody else, both of 1.1.0, in the
    week the first messages went out. They may be two people, one person, or the publisher on
    another device; the records cannot say.
  - "Computers, at least 21", shown on the website until 9 Oct, was wrong. It came from 1.0.2's
    22 downloads, 17 of which were the build check.

WHAT WAS CHANGED SO THIS CANNOT BUILD UP AGAIN
  - The build check takes 1.0.2 from a private copy (ci-fixtures), fingerprint-checked.
  - The app sends nothing from a build machine or from a copy run from source.
  - No test may run the real install command or download from the public releases.
